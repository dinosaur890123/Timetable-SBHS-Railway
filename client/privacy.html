Privacy

Synchron is a third-party app that connects to the SBHS student portal. This page explains exactly what data is used, where it lives, and how to delete it.

SBHS API data — timetable, notices, awards & attendance

Who fetches it. Your browser fetches timetable, notice, award, and attendance data directly from student.sbhs.net.au using your own access token. The Synchron server never sees or touches this data.

Where it’s stored. Only on your device — in localStorage and the browser’s Service Worker cache — so the app works offline and loads instantly. It is never sent to or stored on the Synchron server.

SBHS terms compliance. Storing SBHS API results on a central server is prohibited by SBHS developer terms. Synchron complies: all SBHS data lives only in your browser.

When it’s cleared. Signing out wipes localStorage and the Service Worker cache. Signing in as a different student also clears the previous student’s data before anything new is loaded.

Authentication tokens

Access token. Your SBHS access token is stored only in localStorage. It is sent to the Synchron server only to validate your identity on each API request — the server reads it, verifies it with SBHS, and immediately discards it. It is never written to any database.

Token exchange. SBHS uses an OAuth 2 flow. Your browser sends an authorisation code to the Synchron server, which adds the client secret and exchanges it for a token with SBHS. This keeps the client secret out of the browser. The resulting tokens are sent back to your device; the server holds nothing after the exchange.

Clearing tokens. Signing out removes all tokens from localStorage immediately. The server has nothing to clear.

What the Synchron server stores

Your account record. When you first sign in, Synchron creates an account row containing your student ID, name, email, and year group — sourced from the SBHS identity token. This is used to show your name in the admin panel, track your role, and sync preferences across devices.

Preferences. Theme, accent colour, ambient light, and similar UI settings are synced server-side so they carry across devices.

Canvas links. Class-to-Canvas URL mappings you create are stored server-side so they sync across your devices and can contribute to community suggestions for your class. Only you can read or modify your own links.

Feedback tickets. Feedback you submit is stored so the dev team can review and respond. Archived tickets are automatically deleted after 90 days.

Activity logs. Server-side events such as sign-ins, preference changes, and Canvas link updates are logged for security auditing. Logs older than 30 days are pruned on demand by administrators.

Data at a glance

  • Timetable, notices, awards, attendance: your device only; cleared on sign-out.
  • SBHS tokens: localStorage only; removed on sign-out.
  • Account, preferences, Canvas links, feedback: Synchron server; deletable through Settings → Privacy or sign-out where noted.

Deleting your data

Signing out. Signs you out and immediately wipes your tokens, timetable cache, and all offline data from this device. Your account record and Canvas links remain on the server so they’re ready when you sign back in.

Requesting full deletion. Sign in to Synchron and choose Delete account in Settings → Privacy to remove your account and associated server-side data. Admin account deletion requests are reviewed before removal.

Offline & PWA usage

If you install Synchron as a PWA, your timetable and other SBHS data are cached on your device so the app works without a network. This cache is stored by your browser’s Service Worker — it never leaves your device. Signing out clears it.
